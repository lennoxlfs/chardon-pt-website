Our Methodology

You're Stuck Between Pain and Performance.

Other PT gets you "mostly better" — pain down, moving again — then cuts you off. Six months later you're back where you started. No foundation. No real change. We built the Life Performance Method to finish what rushed care starts. A repeatable path that takes you all the way from pain to actually strong, resilient, and able to trust your body again — even when life gets hard.

The Problem

Most PT gets you halfway there.

Insurance-driven care gets you to "mostly better" — pain is down, you're moving again — and then cuts you off. Six months later, you're back where you started. No foundation. No real change.

The Life Performance Method exists to finish what other places start. It takes you past symptom relief to actual strength, resilience, and the ability to handle your life without flaring up again. That's why we built it, and that's what you get: not a visit count, but a path all the way to the finish line.

01

Pain Management

Get out of pain. Restore range of motion. Create the conditions for real rehab.

Phase 1 of 3

Step 1: Share Your Story

You sit down with a Doctor of Physical Therapy who actually listens. We learn what's hurting, what you've already tried, and what you want to get back to. This is where we build trust and understanding.

Clinically, we use manual therapy, dry needling (when indicated), targeted mobility work, and strategic loading to reduce pain and restore baseline range of motion. The goal: get the fire out so real rehab can begin.

What we use

Manual therapy — joint mobilization and soft-tissue work
Dry needling (CIDN) for stubborn trigger points
Targeted mobility work to restore ROM
Pain-science education — understand what's actually going on
You'll know you're ready for phase 2 when pain is down to a 2/10 or lower, you've got workable range of motion, and the movement triggers you were avoiding feel approachable again.
Phase 2 of 3

Step 2: Get a Comprehensive Care Plan

We build a plan around your body, your goals, and your life — not insurance codes. Hands-on care, dry needling when it helps, and individualized loading that progresses as you do. Every rep is coached. Every set is observed.

This is where most clinics hand you a sheet and disappear. We don't. Your therapist is coaching every rep, loading at the right dose for your tissue right now, and adjusting the plan every visit based on what your body tells us. This is where real change happens.

What this looks like

Progressive strength training — correct load, correct dose
Motor control work — the right muscle, the right timing
Blood flow restriction (when low-load is called for)
Movement retraining for sport-, job-, or life-specific tasks
You'll know you're ready for phase 3 when you can load the tissue at near-normal demand, your mechanics hold up under fatigue, and you're hitting the movement milestones we set at evaluation.
02

We Move You

Therapist-guided strengthening, motor control, and movement retraining. Every rep coached.

03

You Move You

Transition to independence. Own the plan. Leave with the knowledge to keep yourself strong.

Phase 3 of 3

Step 3: Work With an Accountable Guide

You see the same clinician every visit. We track progress, adjust the plan, and hand you the tools to keep your gains long after the visits end. This is where most care fails — but not ours.

You leave with a written program you own, a progression plan, early-warning signs to watch for, and a standing invitation to come back for a quick tune-up if life happens. No restarting. No carousel. One guide all the way through.

What you leave with

A personalized maintenance program you can run yourself
Clear progression plan — when and how to increase load
Early warning signs — what to watch for, and when to call us
An open door — quick tune-ups when life happens
You're done when you've hit your goals, you understand why your body did what it did, and you have the tools to handle the next thing life throws at you.
Why It Works

Built to Finish What Others Start

Every choice we made — cash-based (no insurance codes), full hour (no clock-watching), same therapist every visit (no aide carousel) — exists to make this framework actually work. It's not three phases on a slide. It's three phases of care that take you all the way to the finish line.

Full hour, every time

We don't skip through phase 1 and dump you into phase 2 because the clock ran out. Every phase gets the time it actually takes.

Same therapist, start to finish

Nobody has to "catch up" on your case. The person who started you is the person who finishes you.

Clear graduation criteria

You always know where you are in the plan and what "done" looks like. No endless visit counts.

Tools that fit

Dry needling, BFR, TPI golf screening — used when they're the right answer, not as upsells.

We've heard your story before. We know how to fix it.

Tell us what's going on — what hurts, what you've tried, what you're afraid you can't get back to. We'll listen the whole way through, build the plan around your body, and walk it with you. Most patients are out of pain and back to what they love in 4–8 visits.
