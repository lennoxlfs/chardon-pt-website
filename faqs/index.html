Still Have Questions?

Get answers, straight up.

Do you take insurance?

We're an out-of-network, cash-based practice. You pay at the time of visit — we don't bill insurance directly. We provide a superbill you can submit to your insurance for potential out-of-network reimbursement, and HSA/FSA cards are accepted as a qualifying medical expense.

This model gives us the freedom to give every patient a full hour 1-on-1 with a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy — no aides, no time pressure, no compromise. That's why outcomes are better and most cases resolve faster.

Why doesn't CPT take insurance?

Insurance plans dictate visit length, what we can treat, and how many sessions you're allowed. The typical result: 15–20 minutes with a therapist, the rest of the hour with an aide, session limits regardless of your actual progress. We stepped outside that system so every patient gets the full hour with a Doctor of Physical Therapy who knows your name and your goals — not a rotating team. That's how you get faster progress and fewer total visits.

What does CPT cost?

Pricing is transparent. Call (440) 299-7171 or email team@chardonpt.com for current rates based on the service you need — 1-on-1 physical therapy, dry needling, blood flow restriction, or TPI golf assessment. HSA and FSA cards are accepted. Superbills can lower your out-of-pocket cost through out-of-network reimbursement with your insurance.

Do I need a doctor's referral to see you?

No. Ohio has direct access physical therapy — you can see a licensed PT without a referral from your doctor for most conditions. If during your evaluation we discover something that needs imaging or a physician's input, we'll coordinate that with your primary care provider or orthopedist.

What happens at my first visit?

You get the full 60 minutes with your therapist. Here's what that looks like:

  • We listen to your history — what hurts, what you've tried, what you want to get back to
  • You get a thorough physical and movement evaluation
  • Treatment starts the same day — hands-on work, dry needling if it helps, and your first individualized exercises
  • You leave with a clear plan, a small home program, and confidence that we understand your issue

No waiting room full of strangers. No handing you off to an aide. Just you and a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy who actually listens.

How many visits will I need?

Most patients resolve their primary concern in 4–8 visits — far fewer than the 20–30 often prescribed at insurance-based clinics. This happens because every visit is 60 minutes of focused, hands-on care with the same clinician. Fewer, higher-quality sessions beat many rushed ones.

What's dry needling? Does it hurt?

Dry needling uses very thin filament needles to target muscle trigger points and fascial restrictions — it's not acupuncture. The goal is to release muscle tension and accelerate healing. Most patients feel a deep ache or brief muscle twitch, not sharp pain. Many say it feels like a relief.

Our team holds advanced dry-needling certifications (CIDN and Certified DN) — among the most rigorous credentials in the field. We use it strategically, only when it fits your case.

Can I use my HSA/FSA to pay?

Yes. Physical therapy is a qualified medical expense under HSA and FSA rules. We accept HSA and FSA cards directly and provide receipts and coding documentation for reimbursement if needed.

What should I wear to my appointment?

Comfortable, movement-friendly clothing. Gym clothes or athletic wear work perfectly. If we're treating a specific area, wear loose or layered clothing that lets us access the joint without you having to strip down.

Do you treat kids and teens?

Yes. We work with athletes and active kids from middle school through college, as well as younger patients with orthopedic concerns or movement issues. A parent or guardian should attend the first visit for patients under 18.

Can I book online?

We prefer a quick phone conversation so we can match you with the right clinician and the right service from day one. Call (440) 299-7171 or fill out the contact form and we'll call you back the same business day.
